## A2. Engine forks in sibling repositories (not shipped here)

The patched engine forks that the toolkit drives are distributed in their own repositories under
the same GitHub organization, each in full source with the original authors' license and copyright notices
intact and a `PATCHES.md` recording every change against the authors' code. They are not part of this
repository, and the notices for the third-party pieces they bundle (gzstream, SQLite and the meson
wraps inside Kira; CalcLoop, the FiniteFlow-derived interface code and the SymEngine CMake macro
inside Blade) travel with them.

`kira` also carries `bootloops-tools/`: two GPL-3.0-or-later Python utilities,
`pyred_weight.py` (a codec for the integral-weight layout of Kira's pyRed module) and
`ffsave_degree_census.py` (a degree census of FireFly's `ff_save` state), which were written by
reading Kira's and FireFly's sources (pyred/integrals.cpp, kira/ReadYamlFiles.cpp, FireFly's
save-state writer) and are therefore distributed there, under the GPL, with attribution to the
Kira Developers and the FireFly authors. `tools/kira-stack` (top-emit, battery legs) and
`tools/ffcapital` (`--census` input) use them optionally through the loader
`tools/kira-stack/kira_gpl_tools.py`, which finds them in the side-by-side checkout
`../kira/bootloops-tools` or in the directory named by `BOOTLOOPS_KIRA_TOOLS` and
refuses by name when they are absent; they are never copied into this tree. The MIT source here
is GPL-compatible; while they are loaded the running combination is under GPL terms.
